About me

I'm Daniel.

I am a language and communication coach based in Zwickau. I work with professionals, leaders and teams in English and German.

My background is in sign language interpreting. Listening for what someone means, and finding a way to express it, is still at the heart of my work. In our sessions, I listen, ask questions and help when the words are missing.
