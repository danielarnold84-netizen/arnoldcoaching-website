Your starting point

Bring what is on your mind.

A meeting that went too fast. A conversation with a colleague. Something you want to explain without switching back to English. We start with a situation you recognise.

If you came for communication coaching in English, the same applies: bring the conversation you want to work on. I listen, ask questions and help you find a clearer way to say what you mean.

How we work

A weekly conversation. A little practice every day.

The usual format: 20 weekly sessions of 60 minutes across six months, online or in person.

Time to speak

We talk about your work, your life and what interests you. I help when you get stuck, without stopping the conversation at every mistake.

Language you can use

We pick up expressions and phrasing in context, while the situation is still fresh. You can try them out with me.

Your daily routine

We build a daily routine that fits your life: a podcast, a few pages of a book or a short conversation. We review it in our sessions and adjust it as we go.

Notes and next steps

Your personal project space keeps the new expressions and next steps from our conversations together. We return to what you have tried and where you need more support.

The practical details

A regular place in your week.

How often do we meet?

The usual format is 20 weekly sessions of 60 minutes across six months. We agree the specific schedule before we start.

German, English or both?

Both are possible. We work in the language you want to use more confidently.

Online or in person?

Both are possible. We discuss what works for your location and schedule.

How do we review progress?

We return to the situations you brought in: what felt easier, which expressions you used and what still gets in the way. That helps us choose the next steps.

What does it cost?

After our first conversation, I send you a specific offer based on what you need and the scope we discuss.
